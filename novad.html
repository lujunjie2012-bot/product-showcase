NovAd

Turn product information into video ad drafts with editable hooks, scripts, AI presenters and voices.

Official website: www.novad.ai

What it does

NovAd builds video advertising creatives from product and landing-page links. Supported sources include Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopee and Shopify, as well as app store and website links.

Teams can edit hooks and scripts, select AI presenters and voices, and create different versions of a product message.

Creative approaches

Depending on the selected workflow and supplied assets, creatives can include presenter-led stories, product demonstrations, in-hand presentations and try-on concepts.

Reference-ad variations and batch generation support the preparation of multiple creatives, with progress tracked for each video.

A practical workflow

  1. Start with a product.Provide the product URL and check the extracted information. Use clear images that accurately represent the item.
  2. Define the audience and hook.Choose one use case or benefit, then edit the script to match the product's actual capabilities.
  3. Develop variations.Adjust the presenter, voice or narrative approach. Changing one variable at a time makes versions easier to compare.
  4. Inspect each result.Review product appearance, spoken claims and captions before using the video. AI output can require revision.
