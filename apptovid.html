Apptovid

Create video ad drafts from an app store listing, a website or your own product assets.

Official website: www.apptovid.com

What it does

Apptovid helps developers and marketing teams produce video creatives around app, game and website features. Supported starting points include App Store, Google Play and website URLs, as well as manually supplied assets.

Creative formats include animated explainers, AI-presenter videos, storytelling ads and variations inspired by a reference ad.

Who it is for

App developers, game studios, growth teams and agencies preparing product demonstrations or testing different ways to explain a feature.

The workflow combines scripts, voiceovers and captions with video assets. Videos can be exported as MP4 files in portrait 9:16 or landscape 16:9 formats.

A practical workflow

  1. Prepare the source.Use the relevant app listing or website URL. Gather clear product screenshots and any assets you want included.
  2. Choose one message.Decide which feature or user problem the video should explain. Check the script against the actual product.
  3. Shape the creative.Choose the appropriate creative format and review the presenter, voiceover and captions where applicable.
  4. Review and export.Check names, UI details, claims and framing. Export the aspect ratio needed for the intended placement.
Apptovid is a paid-credit product. This page is maintained by a team member and describes the workflow rather than promising campaign results. Available options and current plans are shown on the official website.
